# 14 · Regaining control

*The method*

**Regaining control is knowing, not fixing everything.**

![Seven steps from a platform in difficulty to a decision: observe, understand, correlate, prioritise, stabilise, regain control, decide. Each step carries the question a decision-maker can ask and what the team delivers: an inventory of signals, a translation, a correlated picture, a ranked list of risks, a platform that holds, a statement of what is known and unknown, a change plan in order.](../figures/14-regaining-control-in-seven-steps.png)

### Seven steps, each with a question the decision-maker can ask

Observe: what do we see? Understand: what does it mean? Correlate: which signals move together? Prioritise: what is critical, what is secondary? Stabilise: what must hold before anything changes? Regain control: what do we now know for certain? Decide: what do we change, and in what order?

### Control regained means

You know what works and what does not. You know the risk zones and the dependencies. You can tell a critical problem from a secondary anomaly. You know what can be changed without excessive risk. And you hold information reliable enough to decide.

### It is a loop, not a project

Every change made afterwards is observed with the same signals. A platform under control is not a platform without incidents; it is a platform whose incidents are understood faster than they recur.
