### The principles

1. **Understand before changing.** A change made to test a hypothesis is still a production change.
2. **Start with questions, not dashboards.** A metric without a question is noise.
3. **One signal is rarely enough.** Correlate, then conclude.
4. **Same reality, two languages.** A signal that cannot be translated cannot be decided on.
5. **Stabilise before evolving.** Urgency is not readiness.
6. **Regaining control is knowing, not fixing everything.** Know what works, what does not, and where the risk is.
7. **Every critical signal needs an owner.** Information without an owner is not a signal.
8. **Decide on evidence.** What do we know, what do we not know, and what do we need before touching production?
